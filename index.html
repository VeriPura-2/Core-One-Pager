The problem

Food shipments are refused at the border for paperwork, not product. One refusal can lose the whole consignment, and cargo insurance commonly excludes it.

40%of 12,486 US food import refusals in 2025 were documentation errors
39%of EU food import rejections in 2025 were paperwork defects
$600/daystorage and demurrage on a held container, before spoilage

Specialist rejection cover is narrow and has historically cost 2% to 3% of shipment value.

The solution

  • Find the rules for each product and route: 15 tariff schedules, 17 live feeds incl. FDA and EU RASFF.
  • Check every document for validity, authenticity, relevance, completeness and agreement (patent pending, U.S. 64/088,697).
  • Flag errors before shipping, for correction by the importer or supplier; missing papers chased automatically.
  • Prove it with a Consignment Passport, QR-verifiable by port authorities.

Traction

Live trials completed successfully with 3 UK importers, each supplying a top-5 UK supermarket, on routes from three origin countries. Passports verified by UK Port Health.
